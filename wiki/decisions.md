# Decisions

One entry per decision: date, the decision, and why. Newest first.

- **2026-10-21 · Set pieces and realistic out of play, in levels 3 and 4 only.** Level 1 never produces corners or penalties. Set pieces matter for fan stats, about 25–30% of goals, and narrative moments, so TASK-9 (ball-path out of play, clearances, blocks, parries) and TASK-10 (corners, aerial duels, direct free kicks, penalties) add them. Level 1 is the fallback and isn't patched.
- **2026-10-21 · Offside emerges, it isn't a per-team rate.** Pitch control drops offside attackers (TASK-6.1), so counterfactuals never suggest illegal passes. In the engine, offsides = runs in behind (team directness and tempo, runner role, pace, opponent line height: tactics, uncapped) × mistimed releases (discipline, composure, experience, fatigue: subtle). Agents can then cite real causes, and repeated offsides give an `offside_trap` moment.
- **2026-10-21 · Event-exact metrics for agents; 30 s rows only for charts.** MCP tools compute metrics at the requested `t` from data up to `t` (milliseconds), and each moment gets its own snapshot, so insights never cite a number up to 30 s stale and the Verifier checks the same values. Rolling team metrics aren't stored per event: they span minutes, so per-event rows add noise and storage, not information. Agents run per moment, not per event; that limit comes from model cost and quota.
- **2026-10-21 · League re-themed to Colombia (supersedes the LA league below).** 20 clubs named after barrios in 8 Colombian cities, so big cities give same-city derbies and names don't clash with real clubs (which are named after cities). Players are mostly Colombian with South American imports. Kept: 20 clubs and 38 rounds (not Apertura/Finalización), USD (scaled to Colombian-league values: median $0.5m, stars $5–15m), English as the default UI language with all 5 languages.
- **2026-10-21 · Rich profiles drive behavior, subtly.** Player and club attributes feed a capped modifier layer (±6%, or ±20% in scripted storylines). Career ambitions are narrative only. Active modifiers are logged so explanations cite real causes.
- **2026-10-21 · Season history simulated by the engine; per-90 baselines with shrinkage; `player_deviation` moments.** Baselines must come from the same engine as live matches, or every player looks anomalous. Flags need p < 0.01, ≥ 30 min, and expected ≥ 5, keeping the top 3 per window.
- **2026-10-21 · Money in USD. Backstory agent + Azure SQL vector search is the Phase 2 first priority.**
- **2026-10-21 · (Superseded) 20-club league named after Los Angeles neighborhoods.** Mirrors the Premier League's 20 clubs and 38 rounds, gives natural local derbies, and costs nothing at runtime (the league is deterministic). Real place names are fine; real club names and pro-team nicknames are blocklisted.
- **2026-10-21 · Wiki lives in the repo (`wiki/`), not the GitHub Wiki.** It is versioned with the code, reviewed in the same commit, and cheap to read locally.
- **2026-10-21 · Open-source policy.** Use libraries under permissive licenses only; use no real match data; never fork a complete app (rules §4.3, §4.7, §4.11). See `docs/research/open-source-review.md`.
- **2026-10-21 · azd for deployment.** Simpler OIDC setup and provisioning than hand-rolled workflows.
- **2026-10-21 · Batch personalization.** One model call per (insight, language) covers all persona variants. Club-fan texts are generated for both clubs; player-focus texts only for players named in the insight. This keeps tokens within the $100 credit and TPM quota.
- **2026-10-21 · SSE instead of Web PubSub; preferences stored in the browser instead of Azure SQL.** Lean architecture, and no personal data.
- **2026-10-21 · Tracking model: event-coupled engine, levels 2–4, built in layers.** Events and positions always agree, which pitch control and counterfactuals need.
- **2026-10-21 · Languages en/es/pt/fr/ar, generated natively.** Translator is not used in phase 1. Arabic shows RTL support.
- **2026-10-21 · Primary prize target: Best Multi-Agent System.**
- **2026-10-21 · Models only through a Foundry `gpt-4.1-mini` deployment.** Claude/ChatGPT consumer subscriptions have no API access, and judges check for Foundry use.
- **2026-10-22 · Ball drag and rolling constants.** The plan's `k = 0.05` and friction `0.25` made a 15 m/s pass take over 4 s for 30 m, failing the plan's own 2.0–2.6 s test. We use a real ball's quadratic drag (`0.010`/m) and `0.7` m/s² rolling resistance instead.
- **2026-10-22 · Level-3 interceptions are decided at the kick, then played out physically.** A Bernoulli draw on pitch control (as the plan says) picks receiver or interceptor, and the ball's trajectory then decides who actually reaches it, so events always match the frames.
- **2026-10-22 · Decisions see the pitch 0.3 s late.** A runner who releases early can look onside to the passer and be offside at the kick. Offsides emerge from runs × timing × the line, and are never set directly.
- **2026-10-22 · Corners come from defenders' safe touches, blocks, and parries.** Until TASK-10 adds crosses and set-piece delivery, defenders who win the ball within 25 m of their goal get a safe touch half the time, aimed mostly at their goal line (never into it).
- **2026-10-22 · Restarts count for the team awarded them.** The validator credits throw-ins, goal kicks, and corners to the opponent of the last touch.
- **2026-10-23 · Scenario files are TOML, not YAML.** PyYAML is not on the Adopt list; Python's built-in `tomllib` reads TOML with no new dependency.
- **2026-10-23 · Goals track xG.** Saves are `1 − 2.5·xg` (clipped), so an on-target share of about 40% gives goals ≈ xG on average. The xG curve was lowered to public-model levels, shots include pressure, and losing the ball now costs the xT we had. The result is about 27 shots, 2.2 xG, and 2.5 goals per match from open play, with a median shot distance of 17 m.
- **2026-10-23 · Engine level 4 has its own wiki page** (`engine-level4.md`), keeping `engine.md` under 150 lines.
- **2026-10-23 · Distance and sprints are per full match.** With substitutes and red cards, players don't all play 90 minutes; each one who plays a sixth of the match or more is scaled to the whole match. This is the usual way the metric is reported.
- **2026-10-23 · Realism and xT use profiles at level 3.** Showcase and season matches always have profiles, so the realism report and xT training run that way too.
- **2026-10-23 · Goal beats bias finishing, not just volume.** A ten-man team rarely creates chances, so lifting chance quality alone didn't move results. A goal window makes the team shoot sooner, with sharper aim and weaker saves. It never forces a goal.
- **2026-10-23 · Season history uses the engine's own motion tally, not 2 Hz frames.** Exact distance, speed, and sprints, with no frame building (the plan said 2 Hz tracking).
- **2026-10-23 · Slow tests run nightly.** Tests marked `slow` (season budget, attribute effects) are skipped by default (`-m 'not slow'`) and run in `.github/workflows/nightly.yml`.
- **2026-10-23 · Matches are reproducible per platform, not across platforms.** Floating-point math differs slightly between macOS and Linux, and over 54,000 ticks those differences add up to different matches. A seed always gives the same match on the same machine, which is what demos and stored data need. Statistical tests use margins of about half the measured effect, so CI on Linux passes too.
- **2026-10-25 · No pandas or scipy at runtime.** Neither is on the Adopt list. The momentum series is a pydantic model, and the TASK-12.3 negative-binomial test is computed with `math.lgamma`.
- **2026-10-25 · Store dependencies.** SQLAlchemy 2 (in the plan's tech stack) is a runtime dependency. `azure-storage-blob`, `azure-identity`, and `pyodbc` are an optional `azure` extra, imported only when a blob account or Azure SQL URL is configured, so local runs need nothing from Azure. We use the official Blob SDK instead of `adlfs`. All are MIT and on the open-source review's Adopt list.
- **2026-10-25 · Insights and personalized texts are listed separately** (`list_insights(match_id)`, `list_personalized(match_id, persona, lang)`): an `Insight` has no persona.
- **2026-10-25 · MCP SDK 2.x (`MCPServer`), not FastMCP 1.x.** The current SDK renamed FastMCP; we use it rather than pin an older version.
- **2026-10-25 · "Foundry evaluation" means our Verifier on the Foundry deployment, scored locally.** Foundry's built-in evaluators (relevance, coherence, groundedness) don't measure catching planted errors, which is what TASK-18.2 asks for. Uploading results to the Foundry portal is deferred, because that API is experimental.
- **2026-10-25 · The Verifier agent answers statement by statement, at temperature 0.** In 4 evaluated rounds, a single ok/problems answer skimmed past invented causes (recall 0.92–0.93). Per-statement verdicts reached 0.95 recall with 1.00 precision.
- **2026-10-25 · Which half is the Verifier agent's call, not a phrase match (reversed the same day).** A code check for "first half" and "second half" in 5 languages caught "early in the second half" for a first-half moment. It also failed a good insight that advised "extend their lead in the second half". A phrase can't tell when a moment happened from advice about later, so the rule is now in the Verifier prompt.
- **2026-10-25 · One Verifier call per (insight, language) for all its fan texts** (not one per text): about 5 times fewer calls. Failures are traced to texts by their quoted statement; if that's unclear, all of that language's agent texts get templates.
- **2026-10-25 · Retries live in `run_agent`, scoped by a context variable** (`pipeline_scope`). Every agent call, however deep, is retried, throttled, and logged the same way, with no client middleware (the fake client has none).
- **2026-10-25 · Insights cite at most 8 events, and `get_events` returns at most 40 without decision scoring.** The first live window cited 131 events per insight, making Verifier calls cost 46k tokens.
- **2026-10-25 · `Deps.sleep` is the plan's `clock`.** The only clock the pipeline needs is the backoff wait, and tests pass a fake.
- **2026-10-25 · Personalized agent texts stay unverified until the Verifier sees them.** `personalize_batch` checks numbers, length, jargon, and names. The wording check in each language belongs to the workflow (TASK-20), so `verified` turns True only there. Templates are verified by construction.
- **2026-10-25 · `personalize_batch` takes `names`, `minutes`, and `scores`** (the plan's signature had none): checking names and minute marks needs them.
- **2026-10-25 · The window length of a cited window metric is allowed** ("over the last 10 minutes" next to a `*_10min` claim). It's a fixed phrase, not a statistic.
- **2026-10-25 · `recap(ctx, insights, client)`, with `recap_context(repo, match_id)` built separately** (the plan's signature was `recap(match_id, insights, repo, client)`). Tests don't need a database, and the pipeline builds the context once per match.
- **2026-10-25 · The recap cites numbers by ref, and code supplies the values.** The model can't write a number into `key_numbers`, only point at a verified claim.
- **2026-10-25 · Milestones only from earlier rounds.** The season history covers all 38 rounds, so later rounds are the future for a showcase match.
- **2026-10-25 · Explainer metric meanings** (TASK-19 start): every metric has a one-line meaning in the prompt. PPDA is this team's own pressing (lower means harder pressing), and every stat is a value at the moment's end, never a change "since". This fixes the PPDA misreading seen live.
- **2026-10-25 · Explainer at temperature 0.** This gives reproducible demo text. Regenerations rely on feedback that quotes the sentence and names the fix.
- **2026-10-25 · The Verifier checks against `Facts`, not `Repo`** (the plan's signature had `repo`). The tools give event-exact values computed from events (`StatsService.snapshot`), not the stored 30 s rows, so a claim copied from a tool must be checked the same way. `StatsFacts` wraps the stats service, and tests use small fakes.
- **2026-10-25 · Metric refs name the team or player.** `metric:<name>@<period>:<t>/<scope>:<id>`. Without it, a claim with the other team's value would pass.
- **2026-10-25 · The wording check runs only when the numbers pass,** and it fails closed: if the model can't judge after one retry, the text fails. It saves a model call on texts that fail anyway.
- **2026-10-25 · A `players` team metric (11 minus red cards).** The live run kept writing "ten men", which was true but had no claim behind it. Code now computes it, so it can be cited.
- **2026-10-25 · Agent output models are plain; checks run after.** The Foundry client parses `response_format` inside the request, so a failing validator fails the whole call and loses the reply. `ExplainerReply` is the wire model and `ExplainerDraft` adds the checks.
- **2026-10-25 · Agents get names and minute labels from code.** The first live run showed ids used as names and seconds turned into "10:38". `match_names(meta)` and `moment_context` give agents names and `56'`-style labels, so they never compute either (Constitution I).
- **2026-10-25 · The match minute follows football convention:** 55:38 elapsed is `56'`, and stoppage time is `45+N'` or `90+N'`.
- **2026-10-25 · `explain` takes the `Moment` too** (the plan's signature was `explain(decision, client, tools)`), because an `Insight` needs `match_id` and the moment's events.
- **2026-10-25 · Analyst: a surfaced moment must be looked up, or it's dropped after one retry.** The plan says "at least one MCP call per surfaced moment". A call counts if it names the moment or one of its events, or is time-scoped near it.
- **2026-10-25 · Tracing uses the Azure Monitor exporter, not the distro.** `azure-monitor-opentelemetry-exporter` plus Agent Framework's `configure_otel_providers`. The `azure-monitor-opentelemetry` distro would pull in Django, Flask and psycopg2 instrumentation we don't use. Both are from the Adopt-listed Azure SDK repo.
- **2026-10-25 · Agent runs get our own span** (`agent_run <name>` with `moment_id` and `attempt`) around Agent Framework's chat and tool spans, so Application Insights can be filtered by moment and retry.
- **2026-10-25 · `make_agent` takes the client as a keyword** (the plan's signature had none), so tests pass a `FakeChatClient` and production passes one shared `FoundryChatClient`.
- **2026-10-25 · Blank `.env` lines keep the defaults** (`env_ignore_empty`). A `.env` copied from `.env.example` set `MATCHMIND_DB_URL` to an empty string. Settings tests now ignore any developer `.env` (`_env_file=None`).
- **2026-10-25 · License gate allows CNRI-Python** (the old Python 1.6 license: permissive and OSI-approved). `regex`, pulled in by Agent Framework, is `Apache-2.0 AND CNRI-Python`.
- **2026-10-25 · Number checks: minutes, scores, and formations are passed in, and words for 1 are not numbers.** `check_text` takes the match's event minutes, scorelines, and formations as sets, so the Verifier can allow "62'" or "2-1" only when they are real. Words for 1 double as articles in every language, so they are skipped. Agents are told to write stats in digits.
- **2026-10-25 · License gate skips packages for other platforms** (environment markers such as `pywin32 ; sys_platform == 'win32'`), because they are never installed there. pywin32 is PSF-licensed anyway.
- **2026-10-25 · Moments the Analyst passes on are stored** (`narratives` rows of kind `passed`). Without them every re-run asked the Analyst again and, the model not being deterministic, surfaced new moments, so a finished match was never finished. The cost: a moment once passed on is never reconsidered unless the match is `--reset`.
- **2026-10-25 · Replay manifests stay out of git** (`data/` is ignored); `docs/costs.md`, generated from them, is committed as the record.
- **2026-10-25 · FastAPI, sse-starlette, and uvicorn added to the Adopt list.** The Constitution's stack names FastAPI and the plan's tech stack names sse-starlette, but the review's Adopt table didn't list them. All three are MIT or BSD-3 and were already installed through `mcp`.
- **2026-10-25 · The API fills in missing fan-text variants with templates** instead of returning no text. The precompute writes club-fan texts for both clubs but player-focus texts only for players the claims are about; any other favorite gets the deterministic template, which is verified by construction.
- **2026-10-25 · Recaps are served in English for every language** until recap translation lands (TASK-25.2). The cost: a Spanish reader sees an English recap.
