# Realism Report

Generated by `uv run python scripts/realism_report.py --level <n> --seeds 20` (seeds 1–20, spread across the fixture list). Ranges live in `src/matchmind/engine/realism_ranges.py`. They are Premier League-referenced and wide enough to include Colombian top-flight levels. Possession is the share of passes; a sprint is above 7.0 m/s for at least 1 s.

## Level 3 with level-4 tactics and set pieces (2026-10-23, TASK-10.3, engine `l4-0.1`)

Matches run with the league's profiles (club tactics, roles, modifiers), as real matches do. They include phase shapes, pressing triggers, cards, substitutions, corners, free kicks and penalties, and use the xT grid retrained on 300 level-3 matches. Every check passes, including the per-match penalty and set-piece-goal ranges added in TASK-10.3. Distance and sprints are per full match, so substitutes don't dilute them. About 15 s per match with tracking, 5 s without.

Why each failure happens, and where it gets fixed:

| Check | Cause at level 1 | Fixed in |
|---|---|---|
| ppda (3.2) | The nearest opponent presses the carrier everywhere, so defensive actions are far too frequent in the opponent's half. | TASK-10 tactics (press intensity and pressing triggers by zone) |
| tackles (91.5) | Tackle attempts fire whenever a defender is within 1.5 m (1.2% per tick), with no notion of a duel. | TASK-9 decisions (tackle choice scored against holding position) |
| top_speed_ms (7.41) | Speed caps of 5.5 + 2.5 × pace m/s (at most 8) and smoothing keep sprints short of real top speeds. | TASK-8 kinematics (acceleration and per-player top speed up to about 9.5 m/s) |
| sprints_per_outfield_player (9.6) | The same caps; off-ball runs follow smoothed targets instead of sprinting into space. | TASK-8 kinematics and TASK-9 off-ball runs |
| goals_per_match (2.15) | Just under the range; xG per shot is slightly low. | TASK-10 (goals are tuned last) |
